# Values Bank (ages 4-7)

Pick ONE value per episode. Rotate so no value repeats within 6 episodes.
Monthly theme: group 4 episodes of related values for the end-of-month compilation.

| Value | Kid-level meaning | Everyday settings | Wrong choice (gentle) | Song word |
|-------|------------------|-------------------|-----------------------|-----------|
| Sharing | "When we share, two friends can play" | toys, snacks, crayons, swing | keeps the toy, plays alone, it's boring | share |
| Saying sorry | "Sorry helps fix a hurt" | knocked-over tower, spilled juice | hides it, friend is sad | sorry |
| Courage | "Brave means trying even when scared" | dark room, first day at school, big slide | refuses, misses the fun | brave |
| Patience | "Waiting a little makes good things" | baking, planting seeds, queue | grabs early, cake falls | wait |
| Honesty | "Telling the truth keeps trust" | broken vase, who ate the cookie | blames the pet, feels heavy inside | truth |
| Helping | "Helping makes jobs easy and fun" | cleaning up, carrying bags | walks away, mess stays | help |
| Kindness | "Kind words make hearts warm" | new kid, someone falls | laughs, friend cries | kind |
| Gratitude | "Thank you shows we care" | gift, help from grandma | forgets, grandma feels small | thank you |
| Listening | "Listening shows respect" | game rules, mom calling | ignores, gets lost | listen |
| Trying again | "Mistakes help us learn" | tying shoes, riding a bike | gives up, cries | try again |
| Including others | "Everyone can play" | ball game, birthday | says no, friend alone | together |
| Taking turns | "My turn, your turn, fun for all" | slide, video game, talking | pushes, game ends | turns |
| Caring for nature | "We protect plants and animals" | park litter, a lost bird | throws trash, bird is sad | care |
| Healthy habits | "Our body likes good habits" | washing hands, brushing teeth | skips it, tummy ache (mild) | healthy |
| Calming down | "Big feelings get smaller with deep breaths" | lost game, broken toy | shouts, everyone stops | breathe |
| Respecting differences | "Different is wonderful" | new friend speaks another language | stares, friend shy | different |

## Suggested first 6 (pilot month)
Sharing → Saying sorry → Courage → Patience → Honesty → Helping

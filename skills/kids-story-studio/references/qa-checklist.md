# QA Checklist (human review required before upload)

## Story (Claude pre-fills)
- [ ] Exactly one value, named at least 5 times
- [ ] All 6 beats present, 4-6 minutes total
- [ ] Sentences <= 10 words; feelings named
- [ ] Wrong-choice consequence is gentle (no fear, danger, shaming)
- [ ] Premise not used in any previous episode
- [ ] No brands, licensed characters or copyrighted melodies

## Visuals (human checks)
- [ ] Every main character matches the character sheet (face, colors, outfit)
- [ ] No distorted hands/limbs/faces, no random text or logos
- [ ] No flashing or fast cuts; motion is gentle
- [ ] Each scene clearly shows its action

## Audio (human checks)
- [ ] Narration clear, warm, correct pronunciation of names
- [ ] Song is in tune and the lyrics are understandable
- [ ] Volume balanced between voice, music and effects

## Publish (human checks)
- [ ] Made for Kids = YES
- [ ] Title/description/chapters pasted from metadata.txt
- [ ] Thumbnail follows the series layout
- [ ] Added to the right playlists

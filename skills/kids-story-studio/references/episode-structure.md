# Episode Structure (fixed, 4-6 minutes)

Every episode uses these 6 beats in this order. Same opening and closing every
time: repetition builds recognition for 4-7 year olds and makes the channel a
recognizable series rather than random content.

| # | Beat | Length | Purpose | Notes |
|---|------|--------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Opening | ~10 s | Recognition | Fixed greeting line from `channel.md`, same pose/setting every episode |
| 2 | Problem | ~60 s | Hook | A concrete everyday situation creates a small conflict linked to the value |
| 3 | Wrong choice | ~60 s | Empathy | Character makes the "easy" choice; gentle consequence (someone is sad, game stops) |
| 4 | Right choice | ~90 s | Lesson | Character notices the feeling, tries the value, things get better; show the warm result |
| 5 | Value song | ~60 s | Memory | Chorus from `lyrics.txt`, characters dance/act out the value |
| 6 | Closing | ~15 s | Reflection | Fixed question: "What did we learn today?" + one-line answer + fixed goodbye |

## Hook rule
The first 15 seconds must show the problem object/situation on screen
(e.g. the toy nobody wants to share). Do not start with a long intro.

## Writing rules
- Sentences of 10 words or fewer. Present tense.
- Say the value word at least 5 times across the episode.
- Name feelings explicitly: "Mina feels sad." Kids this age are learning emotion words.
- One new idea per scene. Pause after questions so kids can answer the screen.
- Ending is warm and complete; no cliffhangers.

## Length targets
- Script: 350-550 words of narration + dialogue.
- Scenes: 12-20, each 4-12 seconds of visual action (beats 2-4 carry most scenes).

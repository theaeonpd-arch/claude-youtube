# Value Song Guide

- Structure: Chorus (4-8 lines) → Verse (2-4 lines acting out the story) → Chorus.
- ~60 seconds at a bouncy 100-120 BPM; simple AABB rhymes.
- The value word appears at least 3 times, ideally first word of the chorus.
- Include an action kids can copy (clap, hug, wave, breathe in/out).
- Original melody only. Public-domain nursery melodies are allowed;
  never copy or reference copyrighted songs.
- Write singable words: 1-2 syllables, no tongue twisters.
- Provide a one-line music style prompt for the audio generator, e.g.
  "cheerful preschool ukulele and xylophone, 110 BPM, warm child-friendly vocals".

Example chorus (sharing):
  Share, share, we can share,
  One for you and one for me!
  When we share, we both can play,
  Sharing makes a happy day!

## Fixed Huggleberry sound (Suno — reuse every episode)

Style of Music:
cheerful preschool sing-along, ukulele, glockenspiel, toy piano, playful kazoo
buzzing melody, soft hand claps, light shaker, bouncy, 110 BPM, major key, warm
friendly female lead vocal with a small kids' choir on the chorus, clear simple
diction, gentle, cozy, storybook

Exclude Styles: rock, electric guitar, heavy drums, dubstep, rap, autotune, dark,
sad, minor key, distortion, screaming

Signature elements (every song):
- Intro: short 4-note kazoo "buzz" melody, then spoken "Buzz buzz, it's song time!" (Kindle)
- Outro: glockenspiel "ding-ding" + spoken "Big Huggleberry hugs!"
- Same Suno Persona for every song (created from the Huggleberry Theme Song).
- Never put artist or song names in the style; regenerate anything that sounds
  like a known song.

Lyrics skeleton for suno.txt:
[Intro]
(4-note kazoo buzz melody)
(spoken) Buzz buzz, it's song time!
[Chorus] ... [Verse] ... [Chorus] ... [Bridge] ... [Final Chorus] ...
[Outro]
(glockenspiel ding-ding)
(spoken) Big Huggleberry hugs!
[End]

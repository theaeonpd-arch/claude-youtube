# Value Song Guide

- Structure: Chorus (4-8 lines) → Verse (2-4 lines acting out the story) → Chorus.
- ~60 seconds at a bouncy 100-120 BPM; simple AABB rhymes.
- The value word appears at least 3 times, ideally first word of the chorus.
- Include an action kids can copy (clap, hug, wave, breathe in/out).
- Original melody only. Public-domain nursery melodies are allowed;
  never copy or reference copyrighted songs.
- Write singable words: 1-2 syllables, no tongue twisters.
- Provide a one-line music style prompt for the audio generator, e.g.
  "cheerful preschool ukulele and xylophone, 110 BPM, warm child-friendly vocals".

Example chorus (sharing):
  Share, share, we can share,
  One for you and one for me!
  When we share, we both can play,
  Sharing makes a happy day!

## Fixed Huggleberry sound (Suno "Style of Music" — reuse every episode)
cheerful preschool sing-along, nursery rhyme, ukulele, glockenspiel, xylophone,
soft hand claps, light shaker, bouncy, 110 BPM, major key, warm friendly female
lead vocal with a small kids' choir on the chorus, clear simple diction, gentle,
cozy, storybook

Exclude Styles: rock, electric guitar, heavy drums, dubstep, rap, autotune, dark,
sad, minor key, distortion, screaming
